Both ran on the same Darwin 25.5.0 arm64 machine on AC power. Each source is approximately 64 seconds, with its selected audio, no subtitles or filters. Playback is held paused for 750 ms to test backpressure, advances past 2.5 seconds, then seeks to 40, 2 and 55 seconds and verifies decoded non-silence after each seek. Video cases also require displayed frames and all cases require worker cleanup.

The new sources use AAC/FLAC in audio MP4, Opus/Vorbis in audio WebM, VP8 with Opus/Vorbis in video WebM, VP9/Vorbis in WebM and VP9/Opus in MP4. Output retains compressed streams. Peak MSE pending queue is one in every case. Initial paused ranges stay below eight seconds; initial playback stays below twelve buffered seconds. The output is not fully generated before playback in these passing runs. Generated bytes, individual batch sizes, source reads, cancellation bounds, source-position checks, native drop counters and per-seek snapshots are in the JSON.

Audio RMS proves decoded non-silence, not exact priming or speaker output. Source-position error is below 150 ms at the tested seeks, but no independent acoustic/visual synchronization measurement was made. Browser native internals do not expose separate decoder drops, underruns or pixel uploads here. Whole-process CPU/RSS and long-duration drift remain unmeasured. PiP outcomes are recorded separately and are not a route-qualification assertion.

The final visible runs use keyframe-aligned video WebM clusters, preserved frame-rate metadata, priming-aware packet interleaving and the forward-gap guard. Earlier failed runs are retained and must not be mixed with this table. See [integration, commands and limitations](README.md).

These tests pin `mode: 'native', nativeRemux: 'always'` to exercise the remux path.
Their startup timings exclude the automatic planner's separate probing pass.
The automatic-selection regressions are a separate run. No remux-versus-Hybrid
resource comparison has been performed here, and the new packaging options have
not been promoted as universally qualified defaults.
